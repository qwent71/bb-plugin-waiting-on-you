See at a glance where you're needed.

## What you get

- A **Needs you** block above the sidebar thread list, whichever list is active.
- Threads where an agent waits on your reply, and threads that failed, each
  marked in its own color.
- Counters next to the title, and a quiet "All clear" when nothing needs you.
- A settings page with a live preview: markers, counters, colors, icons, time
  format, density, empty state, and size.

## How it works

Everything comes from bb's own thread data on this machine. Nothing leaves
it, and no account, API key, or external service is needed.
